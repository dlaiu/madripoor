# Card Abilities

## Party Leader
- **Charisma:** 3
- **Colors:** All 3 (2 per color = 6 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** 4 dealt at game start (1 per player guaranteed), 2 remain in deck and can enter via card store
- **Ability:** Automatically adds +2 votes to the tile/group score
- **Penalty:** If you hold 2 party leaders simultaneously, both are reduced to CHA1 (but keep the +2 vote bonus). Penalty clears when back to 1 party leader.

---

## Hometown Card
- **Charisma:** 1, 2, and 3 (one of each per color)
- **Colors:** All 3 (3 per color = 9 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** CHA1 and CHA2 versions (6 cards) in starting 60; CHA3 version (3 cards) in draw pile only
- **Ability:** When played on a colored tile matching the card's color, automatically adds +2 to the tile/group score
- **Trigger:** Automatic at reveal — no declaration needed
- **Group rule:** The +2 applies even if the colored tile is part of a group

---

## Pollster Card
- **Charisma:** 2
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Starting 60
- **Ability:** When rolling for this tile/group, roll 2d6 instead of 1d6 and drop the lowest. The remaining d6 is multiplied by CHA as normal.
- **Effect:** Same range as regular CHA2 (2–12) but average shifts up to ~8.5 — more reliable, less swingy

---

## Independent Card
- **Charisma:** 3
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Draw pile only
- **Ability:** If played on a solo tile, gains +2 to the final score (range becomes 5–20). No bonus if played in a group.

---

## Hard Worker Card
- **Charisma:** Starts at 1, escalates up to 3
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Starting 60
- **Ability:** If this card loses on the same tile in the previous round, its CHA increases by 1 (CHA1 → CHA2 → CHA3). CHA3 is the maximum.
- **Escalation conditions:**
  - Must remain on the same tile between rounds (resets to CHA1 if moved to a different tile)
  - Upgrades if the tile or group it was in was lost — group losses count
  - Does NOT reset if the tile/group is won at CHA2 or CHA3 — level is kept
- **Tracking:** Requires a physical marker or token to indicate current CHA level at the table (TBD)

---

## Scout Card
- **Charisma:** 1
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Starting 60
- **Ability:** At the start of the reveal phase, before any cards are flipped, peek at all opponents' face-down cards on the tile where this card is placed. You may then optionally swap this card with one of your own face-down cards on any other tile (public action).
- **Swap rule:** The swap is optional — you may choose to keep the Scout on the original tile after peeking
- **Entrenchment rule:** If the card swapped in was entrenched, it loses its entrenched status upon moving tiles

---

## Mr. Popular
- **Charisma:** 4 (the only CHA4 card in the game)
- **Color:** None — declared at reveal
- **Distribution:** Draw pile only (1 total in the entire deck)
- **Ability:** At reveal, the player declares which color this card counts as. Can be used to satisfy color requirements for group bonuses (e.g. Coalition card)
- **Note:** Does not have hometown or independent abilities — CHA4 and color flexibility are the sole benefits

---

## Disadvantage Card
- **Charisma:** 1
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Draw pile only
- **Ability:** Forces all opponents on the same tile to reroll their d6 for that tile and take the lower of the two results
- **Scope:** Applies only to the specific tile this card is on — in a group, opponents roll normally for all other tiles in the group
- **Trigger:** Dice roll phase

---

## Coalition Card
- **Charisma:** 2
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Draw pile only (groups aren't available until round 2+)
- **Ability:** If played in a group of 2 or more alongside at least one card of a different color, gains +2 to the player's score for that group
- **Constraint:** Must be played in a group (cannot be placed on a solo tile)

---

## Underdog Card
- **Charisma:** 2
- **Colors:** All 3 (1 per color = 3 total in deck)
- **Distribution:** Draw pile only
- **Ability:** If played on a tile the player did NOT win in the previous round, negates all entrenchment bonuses within that tile or group (affects all players)
- **Round 1:** No ability — acts as a regular CHA2 card since there is no previous round
- **Group rule:** Negates ALL entrenchment bonuses within the group, not just on the tile the Underdog is placed on
- **Multiple Underdogs:** If multiple players play Underdog cards in the same tile/group, all entrenchment bonuses for all players in that tile/group are negated
